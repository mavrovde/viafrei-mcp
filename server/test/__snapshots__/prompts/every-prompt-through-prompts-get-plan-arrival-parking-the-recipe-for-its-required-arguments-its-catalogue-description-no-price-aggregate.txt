Find parking at Berlin, arriving shortly.

One call finds the parking; the rest are about being allowed to arrive at all:
1. find_parking at Berlin, radius_km=5 (at most 25) and limit=10. Pass only_with_free_spaces=true only if they said they need a space that is free right now — most German operators publish no occupancy at all, and the filter would hide the sites that simply say nothing.
2. Only if they said they are parking in order to travel on: get_train_departures from the nearest station.
3. get_driving_rules with city="Berlin" and topic="lez", unless this conversation has already covered it — the Umweltplakette decides whether they may drive into the city at all, and parking they cannot legally reach is not parking. Use the city name out of the destination; skip this call when the destination is a motorway or a place with no city around it.
4. If they named the motorway they arrive on, check_road_status for it once: a closure on the last stretch changes where the car goes. Offer the prepare_car_trip prompt when the badge or the rules turn out to be the real question, and plan_departure when the road is.

Then answer in at most eight lines: the nearest suitable facility first with its kind, distance and capacity, then the alternatives. For each one, free spaces ONLY where the operator publishes them — a facility with no occupancy data is reported as "keine Belegungsdaten veröffentlicht" / "no occupancy published", never as 0 free, and a reading that is old is given with its age rather than as "now".

Rules that are not negotiable:
- If a tool reports that it holds nothing for this place, read the viafrei://coverage resource and tell the traveller plainly which data we do not have here, instead of trying a different tool until one answers something.
- Show the attribution line of every result you used, verbatim and in full, at the end of your answer. When a result carries a purpose note in its metadata, repeat that sentence verbatim as well. Both are conditions of the licence, not captions you may summarise away.
- All times are Europe/Berlin. Render a departure as 08:05 (+3 min) and keep each result's Stand / As of line, so the traveller knows how old the answer is.
- Report only what the tools returned. An empty answer is an answer: say what was searched and how it could be widened, and never fill a gap with a plausible station, price, platform or free space.
- There is no door-to-door routing in this version. Do not turn these answers into a route, a connection with changes, a distance or a travel time — name the places, roads and stations the traveller gave you, and say plainly that the journey itself is theirs to plan.

Answer the traveller in German, and pass language: "de" to every tool call in the recipe. Place names, station names and road numbers are never translated.
